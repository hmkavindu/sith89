## 🌐 Understanding URLs:

| URL | What it means | Who can access |
|-----|---------------|----------------|
| **http://localhost:8888** | Your computer only | Just you |
| **http://127.0.0.1:8888** | Same as localhost | Just you |
| **http://192.168.x.x:8888** | Network IP | Anyone on same WiFi |

**localhost** = "this computer" (only works on the computer running the server)

To let others access, give them your IP address (e.g., http://192.168.1.100:8888)
